About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at Iowa State University, advised by Prof. Mengdi Huai. My research focuses on data mining and trustworthy machine learning, particularly the privacy, fairness, robustness, and explainability of machine learning systems.

Before joining Iowa State, I earned an MS in Computer Science from Michigan Technological University and a BS in Computer Science from Lake Superior State University.

Research interests: machine unlearning, privacy and security, robust machine learning, conformal prediction, fairness, and explainability.
